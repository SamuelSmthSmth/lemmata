From here to Lean

Lean is a proof assistant: it checks a proof down to the axioms, with no solver to trust. Show in Lean, at the end of the tool strip, states your proof in Lean 4 with Mathlib, side by side with your own lines, so you can see what each line becomes and take it from there.

Every gap is a sorry

Nothing is proved for you. Each step’s proof is sorry: a gap Lean accepts with a warning, for its tactics to fill. The comment beside it says how Lemmata checked the step and the tactic most likely to do the same in Lean: ring for algebra, linarith or nlinarith for inequalities, omega for divisibility, group in a group. A step that did not check keeps its red rail and says so: its Lean states what you claimed, which is not true.

What has no faithful Lean, such as arithmetic with ∞ or an indefinite integral, is left as sorry too and named above the table. It is never guessed.

Taking it further

Open in Lean’s web editor loads the skeleton in Lean’s own editor in your browser, Mathlib included: replace a sorry with the tactic its comment suggests and watch the gap close. Download .lean gives you the file for a Lean project of your own. Every example, every pack entry and every example in this Guide is compiled against Mathlib whenever the checker changes, so the skeleton compiles as given.
