Cookies

{{ name }} sets no cookies, and nothing on this site tracks you. It does keep your work in your browser's own storage, because that is where your work lives.

Last updated {{ updated }}

What is kept in your browser

StorageWhat it holdsWhy
IndexedDBYour proofs, folders, snapshots, installed packs, and a cached copy of the pack registry's index.So your work is there next time, and the Library works offline.
Local storageSettings such as the theme, editor size and panel layout; that you have seen this site's storage notice.So the app looks the way you left it.
Browser cacheThe app's own files, including the checker (about 25 MB, once).So the app opens quickly and works offline.

None of it is sent anywhere, and none of it is used to identify or follow you. Under the UK's electronic communications rules (PECR), storage that is strictly necessary for a service you asked for does not need consent, which is why there is a notice rather than a consent banner.

Clearing it

  • In the app: Settings → Delete everything removes your proofs, snapshots and settings.
  • In your browser: clear the site data for lemmata.sous.systems. This removes everything above.

Export your workspace first if you want to keep it. More in the privacy policy.
