Privacy

{{ name }} has no analytics and no tracking. Without an account your proofs stay on your device and we never receive them. An account is optional, and only syncs your own work between your own devices.

Who we are

{{ name }} is made by Samuel Okwuonu, in England, who is the controller of the data described here. For anything about your data, write to s.okwuonu07@gmail.com.

Without an account

Everything you make in {{ name }} is kept on your own device and nowhere else:

  • Your work: proofs, folders, snapshots, the check history, installed packs and their checks. In the browser this lives in the browser's own storage (IndexedDB) for this site; in the desktop app, in the app's data folder.
  • Your settings: theme, editor size, panel layout and similar choices, in the browser's local storage, along with a note that you have seen the storage notice.

The checker runs on your computer too, inside the page or the app. Your proofs are never sent to a server to be checked, with or without an account.

With an account

Signing in (Settings → Account, in the web app) is optional. It exists to keep your work the same on every device you sign in on. When you sign in, we keep:

  • Who you are: your email address, and, if you sign in with Microsoft, GitHub, Google or Discord, the account identifier and basic profile (name, picture) that provider sends. We use these only to sign you in.
  • A copy of your work and settings: everything listed above, except things that describe one device (which tabs are open, which panel is showing). Each record carries the time it was last changed, so the newest edit wins.
  • Sign-in records: when you signed in, and from which IP address, kept by the sign-in service for security.

These are kept by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in {{ accounts_region }}. Only you can read your records: the database refuses everyone else's requests. We do not read them, share them or use them for anything but syncing.

The lawful basis is the contract you ask for by signing in: we need the data to provide sync. Your copy is kept until you delete it or the account.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. You can do the most important of these yourself, at any time:

  • A copy: Settings → Account → Download my data gives you everything your account holds, as one file.
  • Deletion: Settings → Account → Delete account deletes the account and everything synced to it, at once. Your device keeps its own copy until you delete that too.
  • On your device: Settings → Delete everything, or clear this site's data in your browser, or uninstall the desktop app and remove its data folder.

Export the workspace first if you want to keep a copy. For anything else, write to the address above. If you are unhappy with how we handle data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
